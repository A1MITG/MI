    Our manifesto

    Free access for students and learners.

    Continuum Lab was created as a not-for-profit initiative to help students and curious learners make sense of a rapidly changing world.

    Every day brings new developments in artificial intelligence, business strategy, global policy and technology. Understanding how they connect takes structured thinking, and that should not depend on the size of a research budget.

    So Vantage-360 Market Intelligence is free for students and learners. Ask about any company or market, follow the signals, and see how the future of work is being reshaped.

    The goal is not to predict the future, but to learn how to observe the signals shaping it.
